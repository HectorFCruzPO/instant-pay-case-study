# Evidence to add in the next revision

The published case study has a clear product leadership narrative and selected outcome measures. These additions would strengthen a recruiter's ability to assess the work. They are outstanding inputs, not claims that these artifacts already exist.

- [ ] **Launch screenshots:** anonymized original before-and-after setup, Wallet, cash-out, and history screens. Label any portfolio recreations and use fictional data.
- [ ] **One concrete vendor tradeoff:** options considered, the actual limitation, the recommendation made, and why it won. Include only details cleared for sharing; do not invent vendor scores.
- [ ] **A customer evidence excerpt:** a real anonymized quote or research synthesis that directly changed a requirement, with method and date if available.
- [ ] **Delivery scope:** discovery start, implementation duration, launch phases, and team composition. Distinguish personal ownership from collaborators' responsibilities.
- [ ] **Metric reconciliation:** confirm whether December and 2026 adoption share a comparable population and usage definition.
- [ ] **One changed assumption:** a specific example of how feedback or testing changed the initial approach.
- [ ] **Optional demonstration:** a short recording or interactive recreation of the original launch flow, clearly labeled as a portfolio artifact.

The strongest next addition is visual evidence of the actual experience. Additional charts should answer a new question; repeating the same adoption story adds little.
